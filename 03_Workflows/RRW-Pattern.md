# Research → Reason → Write (RRW) Workflow Pattern

This pattern is used across my projects:

1. **Research** – gather raw information  
2. **Reason** – filter, analyze, decide what matters  
3. **Write** – produce final structured output  

I use RRW for:
- Article & news analysis  
- Tech/AI updates  
- Policy & education content  
- General knowledge extraction  

It is the base mental model for my **Agent workflows**.
